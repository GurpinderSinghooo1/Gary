Feature ideas we can add to any of them

Pick the ones you want and I'll fold them into the chosen design.

One "Continue watching" row across all five appsPulls the resume cards that Netflix, Disney+, Prime, Paramount+ and YouTube already publish to Android TV, with no ad rows.
Background that matches the time of dayDawn, daytime, dusk and night palettes, dimmer after 11 pm so the room isn't lit up.
Soundbar status and controls on the home screenPower, volume and sound mode right on the TV. Depends on phase 2.
Bedtime modeOne button: sleep timer, Night sound mode on the soundbar, screen dims.
Hold-to-launch shortcutsLong-press a remote button to open Netflix or YouTube straight away.
Screensaver with clock and weatherSlow art or photo slideshow instead of Google's ad-filled ambient mode.

How the project would run

Nothing gets built until you pick a design and say go.

  1. Debloat. Turn on Developer Options and network debugging, then I connect over ADB and disable the stock launcher, ad rows, and preinstalled junk. Everything is disabled, not deleted, so it can be undone.
  2. Install the home screen. A and B can be built with Projectivy Launcher plus custom wallpapers and icons. C and D need a small custom launcher app that I'd write and install for you.
  3. Phase 2: soundbar. First try the free fix: HDMI-CEC (Anynet+) on the soundbar, cable in the TV's ARC/eARC port, and the TV's "power on connected devices" setting. If the soundbar is on Wi-Fi, Samsung SmartThings can also switch it on from the TV. Breaking into the soundbar should not be needed.

Based on what people run on r/AndroidTV and in reviews: Projectivy Launcher (Android Authority) · Ad-free launcher review (Notebookcheck) · FLauncher (XDA) · Removing Google TV ads (Android Authority)
